IMPORTANT BEFORE GO-LIVE
1. Replace the LOGO PLACEHOLDER with the official company logo.
2. Replace all illustrative/stock images and product-photo placeholders with approved company photographs and actual medicine packaging. The image URLs currently load from Unsplash and require internet access.
3. Verify the company address, phone, email and all product compositions.
4. Do not publish claims about WHO-GMP certification, in-house manufacturing, testing facilities or approvals unless the company has current supporting documents.
5. Have the business/legal team review the medicine pages and privacy policy before launch. Product pages are general portfolio information, not medical advice or prescribing information.
6. The site does not make therapeutic claims or recommend self-medication.

CLOUDFLARE PAGES DEPLOYMENT
- Upload the contents of this folder as a Cloudflare Pages project (index.html must be at the root).
- Keep the functions/ folder at the root so /api/contact is deployed as a Pages Function.
- For contact-form delivery, create a Resend account, verify the sender domain, then add these Cloudflare Pages environment variables:
    RESEND_API_KEY
    CONTACT_TO_EMAIL       (e.g. support@iftlifesciences.com)
    CONTACT_FROM_EMAIL     (a verified sender address on your domain)
- Deploy again after adding variables.
- Test every page and submit a test enquiry before connecting the live domain.
